## 69 System and program states

**Status:** New.

**Why:** The worker runs on a schedule on the partner's PC, usually while nobody watches. When the PC is offline, the app is out of date, or the account is the wrong one, a day is at stake, and V2 designs none of these screens. After Day 20 V2's last words are "Day 20 done." and "All 20 days done. Pending verification" (`worker_day_done.jsx:68, 99`), with nothing about cash out or what comes next, although the program has an end (`worker_day_done.jsx:35`) and a cash out (the site's $10 minimum, no period lock since 2026-10-14).

### SystemStates (New)

- **Purpose:** what the app says when it cannot do its job, and around the program's end: what it means for today, and the one next step.
- **Anatomy:** AppFrame with the TopBar always (so the app is visibly alive) and the BottomNav whenever there is somewhere to go. The body is one column (`--v2-gutter`, 16 top), centred when it holds a single EmptyState (dashed ghost, icon 24 ink-3, title 16/600 as the page h1, body 13 ink-3, one primary). A condition banner is sticky under the top bar (`--v2-z-sticky`).
- **Variants (all copy proposed):**
  1. offline (nothing cached): chip Offline, no credits. EmptyState offline "You’re offline" / "BlueAI can’t reach the internet, so there’s nothing to show yet. Today’s run starts when you’re back online." + Try again.
  2. error (a screen failed): EmptyState error "Couldn’t load Home" / "Nothing is lost: today’s run and your days are not affected." + Try again, Report a problem.
  3. update optional: Banner info "An update is ready" / "It installs the next time BlueAI starts. Today’s run isn’t affected." + Restart now, over the normal home (WorkerCard, ProgramStrip).
  4. update required: chip Paused "Update needed". EmptyState locked, icon download, "Update BlueAI to keep running" / "This version can’t run jobs anymore, so runs are paused. Update now and today can still count. It takes about a minute." + Update now. It does not name the installed version or what the update changes: no screen shows the app version or release notes yet (an open item in section 76).
  5. not in the program: no chip, no credits, no nav. EmptyState locked, icon user, "This account isn’t in the program" / "You’re signed in as {email}. BlueAI runs on the account you applied with." + Switch account, then "App Player will be signed out too." (V2's former consequence line, dropped 2026-10-13), and a quiet secondary "Get help" (`SITE_FAQ_URL`, the site's FAQ, external) for a partner sure this is the account they applied with.
  6. program complete (Day 20 verified, period not over): chip "All 20 days done"; WorkerCard complete and ProgramStrip.
  7. period ended, cash out open (`cashOutOpen` true, section 41; while it is null the last sentence is "See your balance and cash out on Earnings."): chip "Period ended" (section 30's one rule), no credits. EmptyState "This program ended on Nov 1" / "You finished 18 of 20 days. Your worker won’t run again until the next program. Cash out is open on Earnings." + Earnings (the one Earnings URL, opens the site).
  8. period ended, under the minimum (`cashOutOpen` false): chip "Period ended", no credits. EmptyState "This program ended on Nov 1" / "You finished 6 of 20 days. Your worker won’t run again until the next program. You need $10 to cash out. Your balance carries over to the next period." (the last two sentences are the site's, `onblue/apply.html:2292-2293`) + Earnings as the quiet action. Never "Cash out is open" to a partner who cannot.
     In 7 and 8, when a day is still pending, the body adds "One more day is still being checked and counts once it’s verified." (or "N more days are still being checked and count once they’re verified."). Both use one string set, `V2Kit.endedCopy` (`kit/program.jsx`): the verified count, since cash out depends on it. Home shows this state, not a WorkerCard, once the period ends (section 57).
  9. between programs: no chip, no credits. EmptyState "No program running right now" / "The next one starts Dec 1. We’ll ask you to agree to its Program Terms then, and your worker starts once you do." + Earnings as the quiet action. Dec 1 is a PM-supplied value (`screens/system-states.jsx:176-184`).
  10. left the program (after Leave the program or Delete my data, section 64): no chip, no credits, no nav. EmptyState "You’ve left the program" / "Your worker has stopped and BlueAI no longer uses your accounts." then "Your verified days stay on Earnings, and cash out is open there." (Earnings primary) when `cashOutOpen` is true, "Your balance is under $10, so it can’t be cashed out." when it is false, and "You need $10 to cash out once the program ends on Nov 1. See your balance on Earnings." (Earnings primary) while it is null. `deleting` adds "Your data is being deleted. We’ll email you when it’s done." Secondary "Apply again" (the site).
- **Sizes:** the 419 window; compact 640 keeps the bars pinned and centres the content.
- **States:** each variant; busy on its primary (Try again, Update now, Switch account: spinner in the button, label kept, `aria-busy`).
- **Props:** `SystemStates {state, worker, busy, deleting, compact, reduced, preview, landmark, onRetry, onReport, onUpdate, onRestart, onSwitch}`; `SYSTEM_STATES` lists the keys and labels in order (`period-ended` and `period-under` are the two period-ended variants). The top bar labels here are section 30's chip labels. Settings, Worker settings, Connected apps and Account have their own loading and error bodies (`V2Screens.pendingBody`, `screens/settings.jsx`), not these.
- **Motion:** the EmptyState rises once (180 ms); nothing loops.
- **Accessibility:** the EmptyState title is the h1; error is `role=alert`, the update banner `role=status`; every primary has a 36 target and a verb label.
- **Responsive:** one column at any height.
- **Do / Don't:** Do keep the top bar; say what the state means for today's run or the 20 days; offer exactly one next step. Don't show a blank window or a wordless spinner; don't blame the partner's connection for a service problem; don't show money the program has not decided (D3; the $10 cash-out minimum is decided, both D3 options carry it); don't tell a partner under the minimum that cash out is open.
