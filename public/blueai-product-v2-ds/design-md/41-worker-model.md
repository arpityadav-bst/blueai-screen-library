## 41 The Worker record

**Status:** New.

**Why:** BlueAI is a worker that runs once a day on the partner's own accounts, so its state has to outlive every screen. V2 keeps it in a component's local state, module globals and constants: the day count is a constant (`worker_day_done.jsx:39`), so tonight's popup says Day 7 of 20 and tomorrow's strip says Day 6 of 20 (`:163`); the next run is hard-coded to 11 am (`chat_product.jsx:213`); the mode per app resets on reload (`agent_permissions.jsx:60`); opening another tab unmounts the run (R01, R02).

### Worker record and fixtures

- **Purpose:** the one state every worker surface reads (WorkerCard, ProgramStrip, DayBar, DayDone, JobPreviewCard, ModeRows, RunCard, Home, Activity, the top-bar status chip). Counts are derived, never stored.
- **Anatomy:** plain data, `kit/worker-data.jsx`:
  - `account` {name, first, email}: one neutral identity (Alex Morgan, @alexwatches), never staff data (R39).
  - `setUp`, `coachSeen`, `online`, `paused` (null or {until}).
  - `blocked`: null, or {reason 'no-app' | 'signed-out' | 'no-bluestacks', app}: what stops today's run before it starts (no connected app, App Player signed out of YouTube, BlueStacks not installed). `blockedBy(record)` reads it, and also returns {reason 'no-app'} for a set-up record with no connected app. Decision: a disconnect the partner chose stays blocked 'no-app' on the card and the Activity badge, and its copy says daily runs can’t start, never that they pause (section 63).
  - `apps.youtube` / `apps.instagram`: {status connected | not-connected | coming-soon, handle, mode always | auto, history allowed | notnow | revoked, historyOn}. `historyOn` is the day watch history was answered ('Oct 6' by default when `history` is set, null when it was never asked; the revoked fixture says 'Oct 11'); Account's What you agreed to reads it.
  - `runWindow` {kind time | window | any, label}.
  - `credits` {left, grant 2000, refillAt, runCost}.
  - `cashOutOpen`: the site's answer to whether this partner can cash out (the balance carries over between periods, so only the site knows it); null until it sends one, and while it is null every cash-out line says only where to look ('See your balance and cash out on Earnings.'), never that cash out is open or closed from the app's own count.
  - `today` {date, status scheduled | running | waiting | done | stopped | failed | missed | none, nextRunAt, startedAt, step, stepsDone, ask {job, since, expires}, outcome job | nojob | skipped | expired | stopped | failed | nocredits (once the run ends), error, retryBy, missedAt, checkedAt ('Oct 12, 7:02 am'), ranOn 'always' | 'auto' (once a job ran, in the apps' mode words)} (`kit/worker-data.jsx:88-91`).
  - `ledger`: one row per day {date, outcome job | nojob | skipped | expired | stopped | failed | nocredits | norun, status verified | pending | late | rejected | notcounted, reason, checkedAt ('Oct 12, 7:02 am', on a verified row), ranOn 'always' | 'auto' (on a row where a job ran)} (`kit/worker-data.jsx:75-79`). The outcome says what the run did, the status whether the day counts; Run detail reads checkedAt and ranOn (section 59).
  - `program` {target 20, endsOn 'Nov 1'}. `daysLeft` is no longer stored: `numbers().daysLeft` is the calendar days from `today.date` to `endsOn` (0 once it is reached; `program.daysLeft` is used only when the dates cannot be read), and today's slot is spent once today's row is in the ledger.
  - **The day's deadline: 9 pm local** (`DAY_DEADLINE_HOUR` 21, `kit/worker-data.jsx:29-30`). Every 'by 9 pm' line reads it: answering a waiting job, Try again, Run again, a late start, fixing a blocked run; at 9 pm a waiting job expires and the day closes as not counted. It is fixed, not the run window: the window is when a run may start (default 9 am to 8 pm, section 62), and the last start is an hour before the deadline so there is time to answer or try again. Sections 42, 54 and 60 and R06, R15 refer here.
  - Helpers on `window.V2Kit`: `numbers(record)`, `workerState(record)`, `blockedBy(record)` (null or {reason, app}), `creditsDay(record, now)` ('covered' | 'waiting' | 'stopped': with fewer credits than the run needs, whether the refill lands before the 9 pm deadline, `kit/worker-data.jsx:240-249`), `modeSummary(record)`, `nextRunText(record)`, `dayWords(row)` {result, tick, tickWord, hasRun} (the result words from the row's outcome, the tick from its status) and `dayGap(from, to)` (whole days between two 'Oct 12' dates, or null) (`kit/worker-data.jsx:169-286`); constants `WORKER_RULE` {rate 1, target 20, bonus 10, programEnd 'Nov 1', cashOutMin 10} (V2's RULE, `worker_day_done.jsx:38`, plus the cash-out minimum from its rule note, `:6`), `WORKER_APPS` (each with `actions`, every action a paid job on that app can include: youtube watch, like, comment, subscribe; instagram none yet, `kit/worker-data.jsx:37-42`), `WORKER_JOB`, `WORKER_OTHER_ACCOUNT` (the second identity the sign-in mismatch screens name), `EARNINGS_URL` (one URL; V2 has two) and `SITE_FAQ_URL` (the site's FAQ, on the same stand-in host) (exports, `kit/worker-data.jsx:288-291`). The program's words built from `numbers()` live in `kit/program.jsx:177-202`: `endedCopy(n)` ({title, body}: the period-ended words of sections 42 and 69, from `numbers()` and `cashOutOpen`; the body adds 'Your worker won’t run again until the next program.'), `minimumCopy(min)` and `RUN_COPY` {keepOpen, over, checking(pending)}.
- **Variants:** 24 fixtures in `FIXTURE_ORDER`: new, setUp, running, waiting, donePending, verified, failed, stopped, missed, blocked, paused, offline, complete, ended, endedBelow, noCredits, rejected, automatic, nojob, declined, day20, checking (all 20 done, the last one pending, today idle), behind, revoked (verified, with `apps.youtube.history` 'revoked' and `historyOn` 'Oct 11'; Account's 'history-revoked' state reads it).
- **Sizes:** n/a (data).
- **States:** `numbers()` returns done (verified + pending: the count every surface prints), verified, pending, rejected, notCounted, toGo, daysLeft, reachable, outOfReach, complete, latestRejected, last, the 20 `segments` (verified | pending | rejected | missed | future), and `cashOutMin` with `cashOutOpen` (the record's, passed through, never derived from the ledger). `cashOutOpen` is the one switch for every cash-out line: 'Cash out is open' only when it is true, the below-the-minimum line in the site's words (sections 43, 54, 69) when it is false, and 'See your balance and cash out on Earnings.' while it is null. `workerState()` returns one of 15 card states, in priority order (`kit/worker-data.jsx:212-238`): setup, complete, ended, checking (all 20 done, at least one pending, today idle: the worker stops taking jobs and runs again only if a day is not counted), running, waiting, done, offline, paused, blocked, noCredits, stopped, failed, missed. scheduled when none applies. A run that is going, waiting or done today outranks offline and paused, which only stop the next run; what blocks a run (no app, no credits) outranks a way to start one, so the card never offers Try again, Run again or Run now when the run cannot start. In V2 these come from `?start=worker`, `?day=returning`, `?day=next&end=job|nojob|day20|failed`, `?mode=auto`.
- **Props:** n/a. Surfaces take `worker` (the record) and read through the helpers; the RunCard reads it through its `worker` prop (section 36).
- **Motion:** none.
- **Accessibility:** the record feeds the spoken values (DayBar's aria-valuetext, StatusTick names), so they always match the visible count.
- **Responsive:** n/a.
- **Do / Don't:** Do change a count by adding a ledger row; read times from `runWindow`; keep the record above every screen. Don't store a count, a time, a mode or whether cash out is open in a component; don't hard-code Day 6 or 11 am; don't invent a second identity.
