## 45 Job preview

**Status:** New (R07). Chat's waiting state asks paid jobs in this card (decision D17, option A, built; `screens/chat.jsx` jobCard). V2's AskCard ask (section 37) is option B, one constant away, and asks every other question.

**Why:** the riskiest moment in the product is agreeing to public activity under the partner's name. V2's ask shows only 'Watch the video and provide feedback.' (`worker_flow.jsx:51, 119`); the comment it posts is never shown (`:136`); the first chip, 'Yes, switch to Automatic', bundles yes with a standing consent (`:121`); and the cost of 'Not now' (the day) is told only afterwards (`:149`).

### JobPreviewCard

- **Purpose:** before a paid job runs on the partner's account, show which video, which channel, what will be done (with the exact comment) and what skipping costs; after an Automatic job, show the same facts as a receipt.
- **Anatomy:** card radius 16, max 300, 1px line (attention line while it waits). Head 13/500 with the app mark: 'Paid job available' (V2) and when ('Just now' or 'Since 11:04 am'), on `--v2-attention-tint` in attention ink while waiting, panel grey otherwise. Thumbnail 16:9, radius 10, duration 11/500 on ink. Video title 14/600 ink; 'TechLine on YouTube' 13 ink-3. A plain list with 16 icons (facts, not chips): 'Watch the whole video', 'Like it', 'Post this comment'. The exact comment in a quote block 13/1.5 ink on sunken with a 2px line-strong edge. Deadline line 13 ink-2 with an attention clock: 'Answer by 9 pm or today won’t count.' Actions, full width, 36: primary 'Take this job'; secondary 'Skip: today won’t count'; quiet 'Switch YouTube to Automatic' (zap, opens the consent for Automatic).
- **Variants:** ask (Always ask); receipt: head 'Paid job done' 'At 11:12 am', past-tense list, foot with the StatusTick from `verify` (pending by default; null in Run detail, where the Verification card says the day once) and, only when `ranOn` is auto, quiet 'Switch back to Always ask' (shield-check) (`kit/job.jsx:71-74`).
- **Sizes:** max 300 in the thread; full width in Run detail.
- **States:** ask (partner present); waiting (since 11:04 am, expires 9 pm: the Windows notification brings the partner here, R08); taking (primary loading, others disabled); answered (`answer` set: the deadline line and the actions go, the head loses its attention tint, and one line takes their place: 'You took this job.', 'You skipped this job. Today won’t count toward your 20 days.' with a not-counted tick, or 'Automatic is on for YouTube, so I took this job.'; focus moves to the card, tabIndex -1 and `data-v2-anchor`, as the AskCard does, `kit/job.jsx:58-64, 125-127`); expired ('No answer by 9 pm, so I skipped the job. Today doesn’t count toward your 20 days.', `DAY_COPY.expired` + `DAY_COPY.notCounted`, with a not-counted tick, no actions); receipt. V2 reaches only its plain ask: `?day=next&end=job` on Always ask.
- **Props:** `job` {app, label, video {title, channel, duration, thumb}, actions [watch | like | comment | subscribe], comment}, `mode` (ask | receipt), `waitingSince`, `expiresAt`, `expired`, `doneAt`, `taking`, `verify`, `ranOn`, `answer` ('once' | 'deny' | 'always': the answered state), `onTake`, `onSkip`, `onStopAsking`, `onChangeMode`. Fixture: `V2Kit.WORKER_JOB`.
- **Motion:** none beyond the Button's loading spinner.
- **Accessibility:** an article labelled by its head; the list is named 'What I’ll do' / 'What I did'; the thumbnail is decorative; every action 36 tall; consequences are words on the controls, never colour alone.
- **Responsive:** single column; buttons stay full width.
- **Do / Don't:** Do show the exact comment before agreement; put the cost on the skip button; keep Automatic a separate, quieter action through the consent. Don't bundle yes with Automatic; don't tell the cost after the tap; don't draw facts as chips.
