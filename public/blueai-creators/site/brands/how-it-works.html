Risk, stated plainly

We carry the fraud risk. Not you.

  • Billed on the 72-hour reading, never the peak. The 24h/72h/7d read schedule is the clawback buffer — no withheld reserves, no arguments about a number that later dropped.
  • Bought views come off your bill. Velocity anomalies, engagement outside the band, audience-geo mismatch — anything Verifier can't stand behind, you aren't invoiced for. Escrow returns it.
  • The policy is published, not fine print. Checks, thresholds and consequences live at Verification & disclosure where you can cite them to your own legal team.
  • You set the floor. Approve every creator by hand, or set a trust score and follower minimum and let it run.
