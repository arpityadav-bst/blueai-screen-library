Privacy

The short version, which is also the long version. Draft · pre-launch · 2026-08-03

What we read

Public profile data only. When you paste a handle, Scout reads what anyone can see: follower count, view counts, post history, bio. Nothing private, nothing behind a login of yours.

What we store

For creators on the waitlist: your email, the public metrics above, your three profile answers, and referral links. For brands: a work email, company name, and the campaign spec you assembled. No bank details, no card numbers, no government IDs, no private account data. Payment details at launch live with Stripe, not us.

What a "connected channel" can and cannot do

The token you grant is read-only. It can verify a post ran and read its metrics. It cannot post, message, comment, follow, or change anything on your account — the platforms' own permission systems enforce this, not just our promise.

What we never do

Sell your data. Post as you. Email a drip campaign — the waitlist gets one launch email. Show your handle or face to brands before you're matched; supply is anonymised until a reservation.
