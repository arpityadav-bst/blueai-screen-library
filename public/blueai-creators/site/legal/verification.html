Legal · published policy

Verification & disclosure

This policy is published deliberately. It protects creators from arbitrary clawbacks and brands from paying for outcomes that never happened — and it only works if both sides can cite it. Draft · pre-launch · 2026-08-03

1 · What gets verified

Every billable outcome is confirmed on a live screen by Verifier — not inferred from an API. A post that cannot be observed running does not exist for billing purposes.

2 · When metrics are read

At 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days after posting. Creators are paid, and brands are billed, on the 72-hour reading — never the peak. The 7-day read is the audit trail; the gap is the clawback buffer, which is why no reserve is withheld from creators.

3 · Fraud signals screened on every post

View-velocity anomalies · engagement-to-view ratios outside the account’s normal band · comment authenticity · sudden follower-growth spikes · audience-geo mismatch against the claimed metro.

4 · The three consequences, in order

a. Flag and hold — the creator is notified and has 48 hours to respond before anything else happens.
b. Partial payout — only organically attributable views are paid.
c. Removal and forfeiture of pending balance — repeat or deliberate cases only.

5 · The guarantee

A brand is never invoiced for an outcome Blue AI could not verify. Escrow returns it. Bought-view risk sits with Blue AI, not the brand; each creator carries only their own conduct.

6 · Ad disclosure

The legally required disclosure is attached to every brief automatically and its presence is checked at verification. A creator is never asked to skip it; a brand is never able to.
