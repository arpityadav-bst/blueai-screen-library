Creator terms

Draft · pre-launch · 2026-08-03

Payout

You keep 100% of the quoted payout on every job — Blue AI's 20% fee is charged to the brand on top, never deducted from you. Payment is triggered by the 72-hour verification reading. Withdraw any balance over $10: standard bank transfer free (1–2 days), instant debit 1.5%, PayPal supported, automatic weekly sweep otherwise.

Tax

A W-9 is collected at $600 cumulative earnings; a 1099-NEC is issued at year end. Both run inside the Stripe payout flow.

Content rights — three tiers

Tier 1 (included): the brand may reshare your post on its owned social and website, with credit. You keep ownership.
Tier 2 (+40% of your payout): a 12-month paid-media licence — the brand can run the post as an ad or whitelist your handle. You still own it.
Tier 3 (~+150%, negotiated): exclusive assignment.
In every tier, the post stays live on your own feed. No tier is ever applied retroactively — the tier is part of the job you accept.

What we never do

Post as you, message as you, or touch your account — the connection is read-only. Deduct fees from your quote. Hold a reserve against your balance (the 72-hour/7-day read gap is the buffer instead). Penalize you for declining jobs — matching is opt-in, always.

Fair-play obligations

Post what you accepted, keep the automatic disclosure attached, and don't buy views — the verification policy covers what happens if screening flags something, starting with notice and a 48-hour window to respond.
