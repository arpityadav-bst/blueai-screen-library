Pre-launch status

Blue AI is pre-launch. Joining the creator waitlist or reserving a founding-brand slot is not a contract to supply, buy, or run anything — it is a declaration of interest that holds a place in a queue. No money changes hands before launch; brand budgets fund escrow only when a campaign actually starts.

Illustrative figures

Every dollar figure on this site — rates, estimates, forecasts, comparisons — is a model built for design and planning, not a researched rate card or an offer. Real rates are set at launch and shown per job, per the three-numbers rule (earn / verified by / paid by).

Conduct

Creators must follow platform rules and the verification & disclosure policy. Brands must not request undisclosed promotion — disclosure is attached automatically and cannot be waived by either side.

The boring parts

Operated by now.gg, Inc. Governing law, liability and dispute terms follow the BlueStacks master terms and will be finalized with counsel before launch; this draft exists so the structure is public early rather than appearing fully-formed on launch day.
