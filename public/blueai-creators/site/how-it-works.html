For creators · how it works

The whole thing, including the part everyone else keeps vague

Most creator programmes go quiet exactly where it matters: how much, how often, by what method. Here is all of it.

  1. Paste your handle Blue AI reads your public account — followers, median views, niche, city. You never type your follower count, and nothing connects yet. You get an earnings estimate with the working shown, so you can check it against what you know about your own account.
  2. Connect the channel — read-only A read-only token is what lets us verify a post actually ran and count its views. Blue AI can never post as you, message as you, or change anything on your account. That's a hard line, and it's written into the privacy policy, not just this page.
  3. Claim a job — every job shows three numbers first Before you accept anything you see you earn $X · verified by DATE · paid by DATE. Deals come to you, matched to your channel, city and niche. Approve what you like, ignore the rest.
  4. Post, verify, get paid Your post is read on a real screen at 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days. You're paid on the 72-hour reading — not the peak, so nobody argues about a number that later dropped. Payout is available immediately on verification.
The money, precisely

When and how you actually get paid

  • Rails: Stripe. Payouts run on Stripe Connect — the same rails Shopify and Lyft pay out on. Onboarding, identity and tax forms are handled inside it.
  • Withdraw any time over $10. Standard bank transfer is free and lands in 1–2 days. Instant to a debit card costs 1.5%. PayPal supported.
  • Auto-sweep if you forget. Anything sitting in your balance sweeps to your bank weekly.
  • Tax, handled like a real job. W-9 collected once you've earned $600 cumulative; 1099-NEC issued at year end.
  • You keep 100% of your quote. Blue AI's 20% fee is charged to the brand on top — never deducted from you.

What "verified" means

Checked on a real screen, not an API promise

Verifier — one of the agents — confirms every post live on screen, reads the metrics at 24h/72h/7d, and screens for bought views: velocity spikes, engagement outside the normal band, audience that doesn't match your city.

If something looks wrong you're notified and get 48 hours to respond before anything else happens. The full policy — checks, consequences, the lot — is published at Verification & disclosure, because a policy you can cite beats one you have to trust.

Ad disclosure is automatic. Every brief ships with the required disclosure attached. You never have to remember it, and a brand can never ask you to skip it.
