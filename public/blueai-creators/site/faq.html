For creators
How much can I actually earn?

Depends on your median views, not your follower count. A 3,200-follower food account with 4,800 median reel views models to roughly $141–$235 a month at 2 sponsored reels, 2 TikToks and a few micro-actions. Paste your handle on the estimate page and you'll see your own range with the working shown. All rates are illustrative until launch.

Is it really cash? Not points, credits or gift cards?

Cash, via Stripe. Bank transfer free in 1–2 days, instant to a debit card for 1.5%, PayPal supported. Withdraw anything over $10 whenever you like. The whole reason this exists is that the incumbent programmes pay in $10 gift cards.

Do I need a minimum number of followers?

No. Paid posts default to a 1,000-follower floor (brands can change it), but micro-actions — a $1.50 comment, a $3 receipt, a $5 story — have no floor at all. Under the line, Coach runs a six-week plan to get you over it. Nobody gets bounced.

Can Blue AI post as me, or message people from my account?

No, structurally. The channel connection is a read-only token — it can verify a post ran and read its metrics, nothing else. It's written into the privacy policy, not just marketing copy.

When exactly do I get paid?

Your post is read at 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days; you're paid on the 72-hour reading. Payout is available immediately on verification and lands in your bank within about 3 days. Every job shows you the three numbers — earn, verified-by, paid-by — before you accept it.

What about taxes?

Handled like a real job: a W-9 is collected once you pass $600 cumulative earnings, and a 1099-NEC is issued at year end. All inside the payout flow.

Do I have to promote things I don't like?

Never. Deals come to you matched to your niche and city; you approve or ignore each one. One of the three profile questions is literally "what won't you promote."

For brands
What if a post underperforms?

You pay per verified outcome — views, posts or actions — so an underperforming post simply costs you less. Unspent budget returns from escrow at campaign end. There's no flat fee for a promise.

Who carries the risk of bought views or fraud?

We do. Every post is verified on a live screen and screened for velocity anomalies, engagement outside the normal band, and audience-geo mismatch. You're billed on the 72-hour reading, and never for an outcome we couldn't verify. The full policy is published at Verification & disclosure.

Can I approve who represents my brand?

Yes — approve every creator individually, or set a trust-score and follower floor and auto-approve above it. Both are per-campaign settings.

Who owns the content?

Rights come in three priced tiers: reshare on your owned channels is included; a 12-month paid-media licence is +40% of the creator's payout; full buyout is negotiated (~+150%). The creator always keeps the post live on their own feed — that's what keeps it reading as genuine.

When do I actually pay?

$0 today. At launch your budget funds an escrow; it's released per verified outcome and anything unspent comes back. Blue AI's 20% fee is a visible line item on top of creator payouts — never buried in the CPM.

Why isn't Reddit one of the channels?

Because most subreddits ban undisclosed promotional participation outright, and paid commenting can get both the creator's account and your domain banned. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are the launch set; Reddit only ever returns via moderator-sanctioned formats.

Is any of this proven yet?

Not by us — we're pre-launch and say so everywhere. The third-party direction is published, though: nano creators' share of US influencer spend grew from 3.1% to 19.9% since 2021 (Emarketer/WSJ), UGC converts 1.6× better than brand creative (Kale), and programme advocates post at a 30% higher rate (Duel/Beekman 1802). The first ten founding brands are how the first-party numbers get made.
