Questions, answered

Does creating a campaign cost anything?

No. Setting up a campaign is free. You set the video, the budget and the flight dates, and spend only goes against engagement onBlue has verified.

Who engages with my video?

Creators in the onBlue network, each from an account they own. They watch, like and comment on your video, and onBlue verifies every one of those engagements before it counts against your budget.

Is this only for YouTube?

For now, yes. YouTube campaigns run today, and Instagram, TikTok, X and Reddit are next.

How do you screen out bots and invalid traffic?

onBlue checks every engagement directly, account by account. Anything that fails the check is never billed.

Can I run more than one campaign at a time?

Yes. Each campaign has its own budget and its own flight dates, so several can run in parallel without competing for the same spend.

Who are the creators, and how are they paid?

Independent creators who have joined the onBlue network. onBlue software runs the campaign on each creator’s own machine and account, under their supervision, and they are paid per verified engagement out of your budget.
