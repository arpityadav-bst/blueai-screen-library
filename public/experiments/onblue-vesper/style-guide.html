onBlue Agentic Design System

Every specimen on this page is the real component, rendered with the real class names from onblue.css, the one sheet the homepage and the application both link. Nothing is re-implemented here, so this page cannot silently drift from what ships. Token values are read live via getComputedStyle rather than typed in.

What that does not cover, and what you therefore own by hand: a component's markup, its copy and its set of states. Those are written here and can drift. Before adding or editing a specimen, read how index.html builds it. Longform reasoning lives in design.md.

How the stylesheet gets here. It is <link>ed. onblue.css is a real file now, shared by the homepage, the application and this page, so a restyle of anything specimened here lands on all three at once with nothing to propagate. The only thing still fetched is index.html, for its shared <defs> icon sprite, which lives in markup rather than in CSS. The guide's own chrome is sg- prefixed throughout so it can never restyle a product class, and every specimen canvas takes the product's own --bg so a component is judged against the surface it actually ships on.

Tokens

Every value below is read at load with getComputedStyle off :root, so it is the live value and cannot be written down wrong here. The page ships forced to data-theme="light"; the dark block is still in the sheet and every token has a reading in both. Groups are declared in this file: any token the product declares that no group claims is listed at the end of this section, which is how a new token announces itself rather than sitting unseen.

Type

Two families. Inter carries everything; Instrument Serif italic has exactly one job, the emphasised phrase inside a heading, always blue and always a touch larger than its host so the x-heights line up. Negative tracking scales with size. The eyebrow is the only uppercase and the only positive tracking on the page.

Radius

Stroke and hairline

One pixel everywhere. The only exception is the card sheen at --sheen-w, which takes half a pixel more in light because a dark-to-blue swap on white reads weaker than a dim-to-bright swap on black.

--border--border-soft--hair--band-line--faq-line--hair-2--ruleEvery stroke on the page is one pixel. The section joins take the lightest weight of the set, because they run the full page width and carry far more ink than the same value does around a card.

Shadow

Dark emits light, light casts it, and both readings live in the same token names. In light the button carries three stops: contact, near falloff, ambient body, all tinted rgba(16, 16, 19, …) rather than neutral black, because a coloured object casts a coloured shadow and pure black on white reads as dirt.

Motion

One easing curve, --ease = cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), on every entrance and every transform. 0.35s is the standard for a state change, 0.3s for links and small marks, 0.18s for transforms.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce kills every transition and animation globally with !important, drops the particle mask, hides the flow dots, shows only the first work label and forces every entrance and reveal to its finished state. The closing typewriter and the spark lens bail out entirely and the model rotation never starts. The glyph field keeps its pointer lens and stops its own churn, because a lens that follows the cursor is a response rather than an animation.

Icons

One sprite, drawn once in index.html as <defs id="defs-ca"> and referenced everywhere by <use href="#i-name"/>. The hero field

Three gradient layers plus two overlays, not a video. The stops were sampled off the clay backdrop of the amb-opening clip, then swapped for neutrals of the same luminance with a faint cool cast and mixed twice toward the page ground, so the light falls the way it did while the field sits within about fifteen levels of the page. Chromatic aberration

Four filters in the injected sprite. #ca-spark and #ca-foot split channels, which is right on black; their -soft twins paint the fringe instead, because light's marks are blue and a channel split of blue has no red to give, so the red side renders near black and the whole thing reads as a shadow. Light runs the painted pair. #ca-red and #ca-cyan are the flat channel isolators the illustration hover and the footer wordmark use.

Not specimened

Known gaps and house conventions

What this guide cannot catch

  • Markup, copy and states. Specimens are hand-written here while the page writes its own. A component restyled in the sheet updates here for free; a component whose markup or copy changed does not. Quote from index.html, never author.
  • Behaviour. The CTA decode, the closing typewriter, the glyph field, the spark lens, the illustration swap and the model rotation all live in the page's scripts and are documented here rather than re-run. Their parameters can go stale.
  • The right token. Coverage counts whether a class is rendered, not whether it used the correct value. A raw pixel where a token existed passes everything on this page.
  • Dark is exercised, but only here. The homepage is forced to light. The Specimens toggle above flips data-theme so every token's dark reading can be read, but no page on the site renders it.
  • Three specimens share #site-nav. The product styles that nav by ID, so a specimen without the ID would render differently from the page, which is worse in a reference than an invalid duplicate. The real finding is the ID selector itself: #site-nav a is 1-0-1 and has already beaten a one-class modifier that tried to hide a link.

Conventions the page is actually built on

  • One token, one value. A colour that is not a token cannot switch themes. Anything needing two readings is expressed as a switch, not as a duplicated rule.
  • Specificity stays flat. The hero block is appended last rather than escalating selectors. Two defects have come from a two-class selector quietly outranking a one-class modifier.
  • Measure, do not eyeball. The hero field's stops were sampled off a frame, the model swap point is solved from the shader's own equation, and the arm's joint limits were read out of its animation clip.
  • Effects are ambience. Everything decorative sits at low alpha behind the content and parks itself off screen.
  • Nothing loops unattended beyond the ambient loops listed under Motion.
  • A dead script leaves a readable page. Reveal hiding is gated on .js, the typed headline engages only once its cells exist, and the WebGL canvas stays invisible until a model has loaded.
